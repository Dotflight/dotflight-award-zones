# Contributing

Thank you for helping keep these zones and partner rules right. This repository holds **public facts only**: what
SAS and its partner airlines publish openly. Dotflight is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by SAS.

## What you can send

- A correction: a country in the wrong zone, a partner that can (or no longer can) be booked with points, a rule
  that changed.
- A new fact: a partner route or rule SAS, an airline or the press has reported.
- A booking report: whether SAS could ticket a partner award you booked with points (the Booking report form). Many
  things SAS never publishes - such as whether China Eastern awards can be ticketed - only show up this way.

A correction or new fact needs **a link and the date you read it**. No link, no change.

## How sure each fact is

Every partner row says what it rests on and how sure it is:

- **A** - SAS's or the airline's own page. Only A is **verified**.
- **B** - SAS customer service in writing. Worded as "SAS customer service told us (date)", never as SAS's rule.
- **C** - press and schedules (news, trade press, airports).
- **D** - the community: blogs, forums, other tools' public posts and booking reports.

A fact counts as **reported** with B alone or with two independent C/D sources - separate first-hand observations by
different people; one blog repeating another is one source. When sources disagree it is **disputed** and both are
shown; with less it is **unsettled**. Nothing that is not from SAS is ever written as SAS's position. Facts that rest
on C or D are checked again every 30 days (whether something can be booked) or 90 days (anything else).

## What you must never send

- seats, dates or prices you saw while searching in your own SAS account or app;
- screenshots of any account, search or booking;
- booking references, ticket numbers, EuroBonus numbers, names, email addresses or anything else about a person;
- names of SAS staff, case numbers, or SAS's words quoted;
- data copied from paid tools or other award search sites (their terms and database rights apply).

Issues or pull requests that hold any of this are closed and their text removed.

## Discussions

Questions, ideas and talk about EuroBonus award zones and partners are welcome in Discussions. The same rules apply
there: no seats or prices from your own account, no screenshots of it, no personal details. A fact that should change
the data still needs an issue with its public link.

## How it is checked

A person reads every suggestion and opens its link before anything changes. Merged facts are checked again before
Dotflight uses them; nothing here flows into the extension by itself. Suggestions may be declined without reasons
when a source cannot be confirmed.

## Privacy

Your GitHub username and what you write are public on GitHub, under GitHub's own terms, and stay up. Dotflight reuses
a booking report only as a count in the data - never your name, your issue or when you travelled. Please do not write
personal details. To have your text removed (edit history included), ask privately as dotflight.se's privacy page
says: https://dotflight.se/privacy#github. We answer within a month.
