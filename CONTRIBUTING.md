# Contributing

Thank you for helping keep these zones and partner rules right. This repository holds **public facts only**: what
SAS and its partner airlines publish openly. Dotflight is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by SAS.

## What you can send

- A correction: a country in the wrong zone, a partner that can (or no longer can) be booked with points, a rule
  that changed.
- A new fact: a partner route or rule SAS or the airline has announced publicly.

Every suggestion needs **a public link** (SAS's own page first; an airline's own page or press release next; a
newspaper or travel site only with a date) **and the date you read it**. No link, no change.

## What you must never send

- seats, dates or prices you saw in your own SAS account or app, and screenshots of them;
- booking references, EuroBonus numbers, names, email addresses or anything else about a person;
- data copied from paid tools or other award search sites (their terms and database rights apply);
- anything SAS or a partner has not published openly.

Issues or pull requests that hold any of this are closed and their text removed.

## How it is checked

A person reads every suggestion and opens its link before anything changes. Merged facts are checked again before
Dotflight uses them; nothing here flows into the extension by itself. Suggestions may be declined without reasons
when a source cannot be confirmed.

## Privacy

Your GitHub username and what you write are public on GitHub, under GitHub's own terms. Dotflight keeps nothing else
about you. Please do not write personal details in an issue.
